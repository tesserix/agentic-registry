# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to
the community leaders responsible for enforcement at **conduct@agentic.dev**. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

For the full text — including Enforcement Guidelines and the four-tier impact ladder
(Correction → Warning → Temporary Ban → Permanent Ban) — see the linked Contributor
Covenant v2.1.
